The person behind the counter

I learned watches at my uncle's table in 2016

My uncle Jamal had a small repair table in Agrabad. I was the boy who held the loupe and sorted the tiny screws. That year I decided I would run my own watch shop one day, and I have been at it for ten years now.

I remember the first Rolex I ever sold. The customer asked me three times if it was real, and each time I showed him what I had checked. I still do the same with every buyer today.

People call me JR. Call me any time, day or night. If a watch is not worth your money, I will say so.
